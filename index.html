What we do

Building futures, one trade at a time

We fill the gaps between a great program and a great start: the fees, gear and connections that help students finish strong.

Celebrating achievements

Scholarship Program

Each spring we award scholarships to Tech Campus students heading to college, apprenticeships or straight into the workforce.

Removing barriers

Tools & Equipment

Starter kits, uniforms and protective gear, so no student misses out because of what their family can afford.

Empowering futures

Certifications

Covering exam fees for the 25 industry certifications students can earn, from nurse assisting to welding to cybersecurity.

Who we are

A foundation for the careers Lake County runs on

The Lake County Tech Campus Foundation supports students at Lake County Tech Campus in Grayslake, a career and technical education school that calls itself "a School of Opportunity that encourages a passion for learning."

More than 2,500 students from 22 partner high schools spend part of their school day here, training in 18 programs and earning certifications and college credit before they graduate.

We'd love to hear from you, whether you want to mentor, partner or simply learn more.
